# Platform Groups

Platform groups are SandBase-maintained collections that contribute tools to a workspace's effective toolkit. A workspace can enable or disable a platform group.

Platform groups are not user-owned Setup Groups: they are not cloned into private groups, cannot be selected as an active group, and are not edited item by item. Add or remove workspace-managed tools through [Installed Tools](/setup/installed).

Connected clients always discover the resulting effective workspace toolkit.
